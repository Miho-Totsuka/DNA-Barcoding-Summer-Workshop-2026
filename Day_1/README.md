# Day 1 — DNA Extraction and Gel Electrophoresis

## What I Learned

On the first day, I learned basic laboratory techniques and the main steps of DNA extraction.

I learned that DNA extraction can be thought of as three main steps:

1. Lyse the cells to release the DNA.
2. Bind the DNA to a material such as silica.
3. Elute the DNA to release and collect the purified DNA.

I also learned how gel electrophoresis and a DNA ladder can be used to examine DNA.

## Experiment

I extracted DNA from a plant sample and prepared the DNA for gel electrophoresis.

## Methods

I broke the plant material into small pieces and added a lysis solution to break open the cells.

The DNA was then purified through binding, washing, and elution steps. I used a centrifuge during the purification process.

After extracting the DNA, I prepared it for gel electrophoresis and compared it with a DNA ladder.

## Results

The experiment allowed me to observe DNA after it had been extracted and purified.

I also learned how bands on a gel can provide information about DNA and how a DNA ladder can be used as a reference for DNA fragment size.

## What I Learned From the Results

I learned that extracting DNA is not just about breaking cells open. The DNA also has to be separated from other cellular material and purified before it can be analyzed.

I also began to understand how gel electrophoresis can be used to check DNA before performing further analysis.

## Reflection

I found it interesting that DNA, which is normally invisible inside cells, can be extracted from a biological sample and then examined experimentally.

This was also my first step in understanding the laboratory process behind DNA barcoding.
